Loom charges for every person on your team. That’s fine for three people, but the bill grows with every hire. CaptureFlow does the same job, recording your screen and sharing a link, at a lower price per member, and the people who only watch and comment are free.

This post compares the two for real team sizes. Loom’s prices are its published monthly prices, checked in October 2026.

## How Loom pricing works

| Plan           | Price per person | What you get                               |
| -------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Starter (free) | $0               | 25 videos per person, up to 5 minutes each |
| Business       | $18 a month      | Unlimited videos and length                |
| Business + AI  | $24 a month      | Adds AI titles, summaries and chapters     |

Paying yearly lowers the price by about 17%. Enterprise is quoted separately.

Most teams start paying because of the 5-minute limit. A bug report fits in 5 minutes, but a demo, an onboarding walkthrough or a code review usually doesn’t.

## How CaptureFlow pricing works

CaptureFlow charges per member, and a member is someone who records. Anyone you invite as a viewer watches, comments and reacts for free.

| Plan  | Price                                        | What you get                                                  |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free  | $0                                           | 25 recordings per person, up to 5 minutes each, in data saver |
| Light | $5 per member a month, or $4 billed yearly   | Unlimited recordings in data saver quality                    |
| Pro   | $12 per member a month, or $10 billed yearly | Full quality, AI chapters and clean-up, custom branding       |

On Light and Pro, recordings stay until you delete them. On the free plan, a recording is removed after 30 days without a view. See [pricing](/pricing) for details.

## What each costs for your team

Assume everyone on the team records, which is the most you would pay. Monthly billing on both sides.

| Team size  | Loom Business  | CaptureFlow Light |
| ---------- | -------------- | ----------------- |
| 10 people  | $2,160 a year  | $600 a year       |
| 25 people  | $5,400 a year  | $1,500 a year     |
| 50 people  | $10,800 a year | $3,000 a year     |
| 100 people | $21,600 a year | $6,000 a year     |

A 50-person team saves $7,800 a year on Light, and $7,200 a year on Pro against Loom Business + AI. Billed yearly, CaptureFlow comes down another 20%.

In practice most teams pay for fewer seats than that. On Loom, everyone who needs to watch and comment needs a paid seat. On CaptureFlow, the designer who records three walkthroughs a week is a member, and the twelve people who watch them are free.

## What’s the same

The everyday workflow is the one you’re used to:

- Record your screen, a tab, or your camera with the Chrome extension.
- Get a share link the moment you stop recording.
- Viewers watch in the browser, react with emoji and leave comments.
- See who watched and how many times.
- Organize videos into shared workspaces for your team.
- Transcripts, captions and chapters, made for you after each recording.

CaptureFlow also shares annotated screenshots as links, with arrows, text and blur.

## What Loom has that CaptureFlow doesn’t

It’s worth being clear about the gaps:

- **Meeting recaps and notes** come with Loom Business + AI. CaptureFlow transcribes and chapters your recordings, but does not write meeting recaps.
- **Apps:** Loom has apps for Mac, Windows, iOS and Android. CaptureFlow has a Chrome extension today, and a macOS app is on the way.
- **Enterprise single sign-on** with SAML isn’t supported.

If your team depends on meeting recaps or records from phones, Loom may be worth the extra cost. If you mostly record screen walkthroughs, bug reports, demos and async updates, you’re paying more per person, and paying for people who only watch.

## How to switch

1. [Create a free CaptureFlow account](/signup) and install the Chrome extension.
2. Invite your team to a workspace. Invite the people who only watch as viewers, which is free.
3. Download the Loom videos you still need and upload them, or simply start fresh.
4. Upgrade to Light when you need longer recordings, or Pro for full quality and AI editing.

There’s no automatic import from Loom yet, so most teams move only the videos they still use.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Loom cost per user?

Loom Business costs $18 per person per month and Business + AI costs $24, on monthly billing. Paying yearly lowers that by about 17%. The free Starter plan allows 25 videos per person, up to 5 minutes each.

### What is a cheaper alternative to Loom?

CaptureFlow records your screen and shares a link the same way, for $5 per member a month, or $4 billed yearly, and viewers are free. A 50-person team pays $3,000 a year instead of $10,800 on Loom Business, and less again if not everyone records.

### Does CaptureFlow limit video length?

On Light and Pro there’s no limit on video length or the number of videos. The free plan allows 25 recordings per person, up to 5 minutes each.

### Is CaptureFlow free?

There’s a free plan with 25 recordings per person. CaptureFlow is also open source, so companies can run it on their own Cloudflare account. See [the self-hosted option for companies](/blog/self-hosted-loom-alternative).
